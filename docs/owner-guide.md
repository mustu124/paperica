# Paperica — Owner's Guide

Everything in the shop (products, photos, prices, shipping fee, policies, your story) is changed from the **admin panel**. You never need to touch code.

Every change goes live as soon as you press **Save**. A small confirmation message pops up when it's saved.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to **paperica.in/admin**.
2. Enter your email and password, then tap **Sign in**.

**Forgot your password?** Type your email in the box, then tap **Forgot your password?**. You'll get an email with a link. Open it and choose a new password.

On a phone, tap the **☰** button at the top left to see the menu.

> **Before launch:** the shop starts with sample products and pictures marked **"Replace me"**. The **Orders** page shows a pink reminder box until all of them have been replaced or deleted.

---

## Add a product

1. Go to **Products** → **Add product**.
2. **Basics:** type the **Name**. The web address fills in by itself. Add a **Short description** (one line) and a **Description**.
3. **Images:** tap **Add images**. On a phone you can take a photo or pick one from your gallery.
   - The **first** photo is the main one. Use the arrows to change the order.
   - Under each photo, describe it in a few words (for example, "Red A5 notebook, open"). This helps blind customers and Google.
4. **How customers get it:** choose **Buy online**.
5. **Price:** enter the **Price (₹)**. If you want a struck-out "was" price, fill in **Compare-at price (₹)**.
6. **Stock:** turn on **Track stock** and enter how many you have. At 0 the product shows "Sold out".
7. **Options** (optional): for colours or rulings, add an option type (for example, "Colour") and each choice. Each choice can have its own price and stock.
8. **Categories:** tick where the product should appear (for example, Planners → Undated Planners).
   - **New**, **Best Sellers**, **Under ₹1,000** and **Personalised Gifts** fill themselves. Just tick **New** or **Best Seller** under **Visibility**.
9. **Visibility:** leave **Active (visible in the shop)** on. Turn it off to hide the product without deleting it.
10. Tap **Save product**. Use **Preview** to see it as a customer would.

**Tip:** for a similar product, open an existing one and tap **Duplicate**. Then change the name, photos and price.

## Make a product "Customise on WhatsApp"

For personalised items, customers don't pay online. They message you on WhatsApp instead.

1. Open the product (or add a new one).
2. Under **How customers get it**, choose **Customise on WhatsApp**.
3. **Starting price (₹)** is optional. Leave it empty to show "Enquire for price".
4. Tap **Save product**.

The product page now shows a **Customise on WhatsApp** button. When a customer taps it, WhatsApp opens with a message that includes the product name and link.

- Anything placed in the **PERSONALISED** category is always WhatsApp only. The shop won't let you switch it back to Buy online.
- To change the pre-filled message, go to **Site Settings** → **"Customise on WhatsApp" message**.

## Change homepage images

1. Go to **Homepage**. You'll see the sections in the order they appear on the site: Hero banner, Shop By Collection, Promo banners, Product feature, Promo tiles, Instagram.
2. Tap **Edit** next to a section.
3. Under each panel or tile, tap **Replace** on an image and choose a new photo. It's resized automatically.
   - **Hero banner and Promo banners** have an **Image A** and an **Image B**, which swap every second. Upload both.
   - Change the text, button label and link in the same box.
4. Tap **Save section**.

**Different picture on phones:** under each image there's a smaller **"phone version (optional)"** upload. Use it when a wide laptop banner doesn't crop well on a phone — for example a tall 4:5 photo for the hero. Phones show the phone version; laptops and tablets show the main image. Leave it empty to use the main image everywhere.

On the **Homepage** list, the arrows move a section up or down. The **Shown / Hidden** switch shows or hides it.

Photo sizes that work well:

| Section | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | wide landscape |
| Collection tiles | portrait |
| Instagram | square |

## Edit the shipping fee

1. Go to **Site Settings** → **Shipping & delivery**.
2. Change **Standard shipping fee (₹)**.
   - To offer free shipping on big orders, fill in **Free shipping above (₹)**. Leave it empty for no free shipping.
3. Tap **Save settings** (bottom of the screen).

The new fee updates everywhere at once: the announcement bar, the cart, checkout, the shipping policy and the FAQs.

Dispatch and delivery times are on the same page, for example "4–6 business days".

## Gift sets

Gift sets are products in **GIFTS → Gift Sets**. Each one lists what's inside under **Gift set contents**, for example "1 × Notepad — any product in Paper Edit → Notepads". The customer picks the designs on WhatsApp after ordering, and the note at the bottom of the Gift Sets page tells them to.

- **Stock works itself out.** A set can be bought while there's enough of every item inside it. For example, with 2 notepads left in total, at most 2 sets that include a notepad can be bought.
- **When a gift-set order comes in:**
  1. The order page lists what's inside the set. Agree the designs with the customer on WhatsApp.
  2. Before marking the order **Dispatched**, reduce the stock of the exact designs you packed (Products → each item). Until then, the paid order holds those items back.
- **Price:** the set's own price is what the customer pays. Put the "individual retail total" in **Compare-at price** to show the saving.
- **Adding a new set:** duplicate an existing set and change the name, price, photos and contents.

To change the note at the bottom of the Gift Sets page, go to **Categories & Menu** → GIFTS → Gift Sets.

## Free gift offer

When a customer buys enough products marked **New**, a free item is added to their order at checkout at ₹0. It's set up as "Buy any 2 from the New Collection and get a set of Snap-in Separators free".

- It's added automatically. The customer doesn't need a code. With only 1 New product in the cart, checkout tells them to add 1 more.
- It's one free item per order. The free item itself doesn't count towards the 2.
- If the free item is sold out, the order still goes through without it, and checkout says so.
- In **Orders**, it shows as "Snap-in Separators (free gift)" at ₹0. Its stock goes down like any other item.

To change or stop it, go to **Site Settings** → **Free gift offer**. You can:

- turn the offer on or off;
- change the text;
- change how many New products are needed;
- change the free product, by typing the end of its web address (for example `snap-in-separators`).

Then tap **Save settings**. Remember to update the announcement bar text too.

## Edit your founder story

1. Go to **Pages & FAQs** → **About**.
2. Edit the **Our Story (keep the heading at the top)** box. Use the toolbar for bold, lists and links.
   - The **Intro** and **Our Mission** boxes are on the same page.
3. Tap **Save page**.

## Update a policy

1. Go to **Pages & FAQs**. Choose the policy: Shipping Policy; Cancellation, Returns & Refund Policy; Privacy Policy; or Terms & Conditions.
2. Edit the **Page text**.
3. Set the **"Last updated" date** to today.
4. Tap **Save page**.

**Words in `{{double brackets}}`**, such as `{{contact_email}}` or `{{standard_shipping_fee_inr}}`, are filled in automatically from Site Settings. Leave them as they are, so the policy always shows your current email, WhatsApp number and fees. The **Tokens** box on the Pages & FAQs screen lists all of them.

FAQs are on the same screen. You can add, edit, hide or reorder them.

## Custom Orders and Corporate Gifting forms

Both pages end with a short form. When a customer taps the button, WhatsApp opens with their answers already typed in. They tap Send, and you reply with the final quote. Nothing is paid online, and the form isn't an order.

To change the choices or the indicative prices:

1. Go to **Pages & FAQs** → **Custom Orders** (or **Corporate Gifting**).
2. In **Form options**, put one choice on each line. Anything after a **|** adds a detail:
   - a price: `Softbound Wiro | 899`
   - `extra` to show the "Additional charges apply" note: `Planner layout | extra`
   - `photo book` for a format that asks about photos instead of cover and pages: `Custom Photo Book | 2500 | photo book`
3. **WhatsApp button** sets the button text and the first line of the message.
4. Tap **Save page**.

## Mark an order as dispatched and add tracking

1. Go to **Orders**. Paid orders are under **Confirmed (paid)**. Tap the order number.
2. Tap **Copy address** to paste the address into your courier's website. **Packing slip** prints a slip without prices.
3. Under **Dispatch & tracking**:
   - Set **Dispatch status** to **Dispatched**.
   - Type the **Courier name** (for example, Delhivery).
   - In **Tracking details**, paste the tracking number and/or tracking link.
4. Tap **Save changes**.
5. Tap **Open WhatsApp** to send the customer a ready-made message with the courier and tracking details. Check it, then press send.

Customers can also see the status and tracking themselves on the **Track Your Order** page, using their order number and the mobile number or email they used at checkout.

When the parcel arrives, set the status to **Delivered** and save.

**Refunds:** make the refund in your Razorpay dashboard first. Then tick **Refunded** on the order and save.

## Changing the logo or link preview picture

Go to **Site Settings** → **Branding & sharing** to replace the following, then tap **Save settings**:

- **Header logo**
- **Footer logo**
- **Link preview image:** the picture people see when the shop link is shared on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook. Best size is 1200 × 630.
